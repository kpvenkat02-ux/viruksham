About K P VENKATARAMAKRISHNAN

Wealth Creator, Ayurvedic Enthusiast & Institutional Veteran

A Commerce graduate with 35 years of rich experience across corporate finance, stock broking, and mutual funds. He spent 7 years with Sundaram Finance Group as Head – Institutional Sales for Sundaram Mutual Fund in Chennai, spearheading key institutional relationships.

In May 2017, he founded Viruksham Financial Services, which evolved into Viruksham Finmart Private Limited on April 23, 2026. Today, he stewards over ₹500 Crores in corpus for 200+ discerning HNI/MNI families and corporate clients.

Beyond finance, he has served as Treasurer of Vaishnavi Welfare and Charitable Trust for over 10 years, actively supporting children's nutrition through daily Ayurvedic ‘Ashwagandhadhi’ milk programs. He is also passionate about Drawing, Photography, and Cooking.
