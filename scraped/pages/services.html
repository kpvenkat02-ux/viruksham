What a mutual fund distributor actually does

An AMFI-registered mutual fund distributor does more than help you buy a fund. The value is in choosing schemes that suit you, executing the transactions, monitoring the portfolio afterwards, and being reachable when something needs doing.

Line 01

Mutual Funds

Choosing schemes that suit you, executing the transactions, monitoring the portfolio afterwards, and being reachable when something needs doing.

AMFI-registered mutual fund distributor, ARN 274361.

  • 01

    SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)

    Invest a fixed amount regularly into selected mutual funds to build wealth over time with discipline.

  • 02

    Step-up SIP

    Automatically increase your monthly SIP amount every year as your income grows to achieve goals faster.

  • 03

    Lump sum investment

    Invest surplus funds in one go across suitable equity, debt, or hybrid funds aligned with your horizon.

  • 04

    STP (Systematic Transfer Plan)

    Park lump sum money in a liquid fund and systematically transfer fixed amounts into equity funds.

  • 05

    SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan)

    Withdraw a fixed amount regularly from your mutual fund investments for steady cash flow in retirement.

  • 06

    Switch between schemes or plans

    Seamlessly move capital between different mutual fund schemes or asset classes within the same fund house.

  • 07

    Redemption (full or partial)

    Easily withdraw your accumulated investment corpus partially or fully when you need the money.

  • 08

    Dividend/IDCW and growth option selection

    Choose between growth for compounding returns or IDCW payout if you need periodic cash flow.

  • 09

    Equity funds

    Invest in large, mid, small, or flexi cap stocks to seek long-term capital appreciation for financial goals.

  • 10

    Debt funds

    Invest in government bonds and corporate debt instruments for capital preservation and stable returns.

  • 11

    Hybrid funds

    A balanced combination of equity and debt assets to offer growth with moderate downside risk.

  • 12

    Index funds and ETFs

    Cost-effective passive funds replicating major market indices like Nifty 50 and Sensex.

  • 13

    ELSS (tax-saving funds)

    Equity-linked saving schemes offering tax deduction under Section 80C with a 3-year lock-in period.

  • 14

    Liquid and ultra-short funds

    Safe, short-term debt funds ideal for parking emergency funds and surplus cash with high liquidity.

  • 15

    International funds

    Geographical diversification by investing in overseas companies and global equity indices.

  • 16

    Goal-based planning (retirement, education, home)

    Structure targeted investment portfolios for retirement, children's education, home purchase, or financial freedom.

  • 17

    Risk profiling

    Assess your risk tolerance and financial capacity before recommending appropriate mutual fund schemes.

  • 18

    Asset allocation

    Distribute your money across equity, debt, gold, and cash to balance risk and expected growth.

  • 19

    Portfolio review and rebalancing

    Periodic assessment to keep your mutual fund asset allocation aligned with your original goals.

  • 20

    Tax planning

    We explain how mutual fund gains are taxed and help you choose tax-saving funds like ELSS. For personal tax advice, please consult a tax professional.

  • 21

    KYC and onboarding

    Step-by-step guidance for KYC verification, PAN linking, and smooth onboarding for new investors.

  • 22

    Folio creation and consolidation

    Create new folios and consolidate multiple existing mutual fund folios into a clean, unified view.

  • 23

    Nominee registration and updates

    Ensure smooth nomination registration and timely updates across all your mutual fund folios.

  • 24

    Bank mandate and contact detail updates

    Quick assistance in updating bank accounts, NACH auto-debit mandates, address, and contact information.

  • 25

    Account statements

    Generate consolidated account statements (CAS) showing your complete investment holdings and valuations.

  • 26

    Capital gains and tax reports

    Download detailed realized capital gains statements for easy income tax filing every financial year.

  • 27

    SIP and lump sum calculators

    Calculate projected returns with interactive SIP and Lump sum calculators.

  • 28

    Goal planner

    Forecast required monthly savings and corpus milestones with our interactive Goal Planner tool.

  • 29

    Portfolio tracking and client portal

    Track your portfolio 24/7 online and access reports via our secure Client Portal.

  • 30

    Transaction alerts

    Stay informed with instant SMS and email notifications for all purchases, SIP deductions, and switches.

    Investment Requirement Analysis

  • 02

    FD Product Selection

    Help investors identify suitable FDs based on tenure, interest rate, issuer and payout preference.

  • 03

    FD Rate Comparison

    Compare rates across banks/financial institutions, NBFCs and other eligible issuers available.

  • 04

    Corporate FD Distribution

    Facilitate investment in corporate/NBFC fixed deposits, where authorised.

  • 05

    Bond Selection

    Help investors evaluate and select bonds based on tenure, coupon, yield, credit quality and other relevant characteristics.

  • 06

    Government Securities

    Facilitate access to eligible government securities and other government-backed fixed-income products through authorised channels.

  • 07

    Tax-Saving Investments

    Help investors identify eligible tax-saving fixed-income products, subject to prevailing tax rules.

  • 08

    Regular Income Planning

    Help investors structure FD/bond investments for periodic interest income or cash-flow requirements.

  • 09

    Laddering Strategy

    Create an FD/bond ladder with different maturities to manage liquidity and reinvestment risk.

  • 10

    Maturity Planning

    Track maturity dates and help investors plan reinvestment of principal.

  • 11

    Interest Payout Planning

    Help investors choose cumulative or periodic interest payout options according to their cash-flow needs.

  • 12

    Portfolio Diversification

    Help investors diversify investments across issuers, maturities and types of fixed-income instruments.

  • 13

    Credit Risk Assessment

    Explain issuer credit ratings, rating changes, security/collateral and other relevant credit-risk information.

  • 14

    Yield & Return Comparison

    Compare coupon rates, effective yields and expected cash flows across available products.

  • 15

    Liquidity Assessment

    Explain lock-ins, premature withdrawal conditions, secondary-market liquidity and other exit considerations.

  • 16

    Application & Transaction Assistance

  • 17

    Renewal Management

    Send maturity/renewal reminders and assist investors in deciding whether to renew or reinvest.

  • 18

    Interest & Maturity Tracking

    Maintain records of interest payments, maturity proceeds and upcoming cash flows.

  • 19

    TDS / Tax Documentation Support

    Help investors understand relevant TDS certificates and tax-related documents; tax advice is provided only within our permitted scope.

  • 20

    Bond Portfolio Management

    Maintain a consolidated view of the investor's bond holdings, coupon income, maturity dates and outstanding principal.

  • 21

    Secondary Market Assistance

    Where the product and regulatory framework permit, assist investors with understanding secondary-market sale/purchase options.

  • 22

    Reinvestment Services

    Identify suitable reinvestment opportunities when FDs or bonds mature.

  • 23

    Investor Statements

    Provide consolidated statements of investments, interest income, maturities and transactions.

  • 24

    Ongoing Customer Service

    Provide post-investment support and help resolve servicing-related issues with the issuer/platform.
