The Story

35 Years of Financial Expertise & Dedicated Stewardship

K P VENKATARAMAKRISHNAN is a seasoned Wealth Creator, Commerce graduate, and Ayurvedic Enthusiast with over 35 years of multifaceted experience across corporate finance, stock broking, and mutual funds.

A significant milestone in his distinguished career was spending 7 years with Sundaram Finance Group as Head – Institutional Sales for Sundaram Mutual Fund in Chennai, spearheading key relationships and managing high-stakes corporate and institutional investments with meticulous rigor.

In May 2017, he founded Viruksham Financial Services to bring bespoke, disciplined, and transparent investment advisory to individuals and institutions. Reflecting its rapid scale and institutional excellence, the firm was converted into Viruksham Finmart Private Limited on April 23, 2026. Today, he oversees a prestigious ₹500+ Crores corpus serving more than 200 individual HNI/MNI families and corporates.

Community & Ayurvedic Philanthropy

For the past 10 years, he has served as Treasurer of Vaishnavi Welfare and Charitable Trust. The trust was created with a heartfelt mission to intervene in the malnourishment of underprivileged children through India's ancient science of Ayurveda. A dedicated team works tirelessly every day to supply fresh milk enriched with the healing goodness of Ayurvedic ‘Ashwagandhadhi’.

Outside the boardroom and investment analysis, he is deeply passionate about Drawing, Photography, and Cooking, bringing creativity, mindfulness, and patience to every sphere of his life.
